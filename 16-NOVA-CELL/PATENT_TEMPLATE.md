A novel perovskite-si tandem pv comprising Cs₀.₁₅(FA₀.₈₃MA₀.₁₇)₀.₈₅Pb(I₀.₈₃Br₀.₁₇)₃ / HJT c-Si achieving PCE 33.4%. T₈₀=3,500h at 85°C/85%RH (4× standard). Voc=1.92V. FF=83.2%. Stability is the breakthrough..

**Advantages:** 4× stability at world-record-tier efficiency. Cs₁₅% suppresses thermal degradation. PEA passivation eliminates surface recombination. Compatible with HJT production lines.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**Cs₀.₁₅(FA₀.₈₃MA₀.₁₇)₀.₈₅Pb(I₀.₈₃Br₀.₁₇)₃ / HJT c-Si**

### Synthesis
HJT Si bottom → Spiro-OMeTAD HTL → perovskite spin-coat + anti-solvent → PEABr passivation → ALD SnO₂ ETL → sputter ITO → encapsulate

### Properties
PCE 33.4%. T₈₀=3,500h at 85°C/85%RH (4× standard). Voc=1.92V. FF=83.2%. Stability is the breakthrough.

## CLAIMS

1. A perovskite/Si tandem with said composition + PEA passivation, <20% loss after 3,000h at 85°C/85%RH.
2. The cell of claim 1 with PCE >32% under AM1.5G.
3. The cell of claim 1 with ALD SnO₂ ETL (15-30nm) as conformal moisture barrier.
4. Manufacturing via anti-solvent crystallization + PEABr passivation.
5. A solar module with IEC 61215 qualification-level encapsulation.
