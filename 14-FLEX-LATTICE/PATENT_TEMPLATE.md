## TITLE
**Auxetic Impact Metamaterial: TPU-85A re-entrant + chiral dual-topology lattice**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to auxetic impact metamaterial, specifically impact protection (helmets, armor) utilizing TPU-85A re-entrant + chiral dual-topology lattice.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: D3O, Riddell, Koroyd. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel auxetic impact metamaterial comprising TPU-85A re-entrant + chiral dual-topology lattice achieving ν = -0.3 to -0.8 (tunable). 85 J/cm³ absorption (3× foam). 94% rebound. FDM printable..

**Advantages:** Gets thicker at impact (concentrates protection). 3× absorption. Multi-impact (94% rebound vs single-use EPS). Customizable per athlete.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**TPU-85A re-entrant + chiral dual-topology lattice**

### Synthesis
Parametric CAD (OpenSCAD) → slice 0.15mm → FDM print TPU-85A (230°C/60°C/20mm/s) → IPA wash → 60°C anneal 2h

### Properties
ν = -0.3 to -0.8 (tunable). 85 J/cm³ absorption (3× foam). 94% rebound. FDM printable.

## CLAIMS

1. An auxetic metamaterial with dual re-entrant+chiral topology, tunable ν from -0.3 to -0.8, 3D printed from TPU.
2. The structure of claim 1 with energy absorption >70 J/cm³ under quasi-static compression.
3. The structure of claim 1 with elastic rebound >90% after 50% strain.
4. Manufacturing via parametric CAD generation + FDM printing with controlled parameters.
5. A helmet incorporating said structure with spatially varying cell geometry per head zone.
