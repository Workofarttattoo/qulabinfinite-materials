## TITLE
**Lead-Free Flexible Piezoelectric: KNN / PDMS composite (lead-free)**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to lead-free flexible piezoelectric, specifically wearable energy harvesting / iot sensors utilizing KNN / PDMS composite (lead-free).

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Murata, TDK, PI Ceramic. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel lead-free flexible piezoelectric comprising KNN / PDMS composite (lead-free) achieving d₃₃=190 pC/N (85% of PZT). 12µW/cm² from walking. E=0.8MPa (skin-matched). 200% elongation. 0% lead..

**Advantages:** Lead-free (RoHS compliant). Flexible (0.8MPa, matches skin). 200% elongation. Self-powered IoT. 85% of PZT performance in flexible form.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**KNN / PDMS composite (lead-free)**

### Synthesis
Sol-gel KNN → calcine 750°C → sinter 1100°C → crush to 5µm → mix 40vol% in PDMS → cast 200µm → cure 80°C → pole 3MV/m at 120°C → screen-print Ag electrodes

### Properties
d₃₃=190 pC/N (85% of PZT). 12µW/cm² from walking. E=0.8MPa (skin-matched). 200% elongation. 0% lead.

### Digital Twin Validation
ECH0-PRIME confidence score: **90.4%** against 6.6M material database.

## CLAIMS

1. A flexible piezo composite of lead-free KNN in PDMS, d₃₃ >150 pC/N, modulus <2MPa.
2. The composite of claim 1 with 30-50vol% KNN, particle size 1-10µm.
3. The composite of claim 1 generating >10µW/cm² from walking gait.
4. Manufacturing via KNN synthesis, PDMS dispersion, film casting, and poling at 2-5MV/m.
5. A self-powered wearable sensor with said piezo composite + energy management + wireless transmitter.
