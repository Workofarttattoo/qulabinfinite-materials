## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**Microencapsulated paraffin / expanded graphite / SiO₂ shell**

### Synthesis
Emulsify paraffin (Tm=28°C) → sol-gel SiO₂ shell → expand graphite (H₂SO₄ + flash 900°C) → vacuum impregnate → compress to panels

### Properties
Latent heat >200J/g. κ = 5-10 W/mK (50× paraffin). 5000+ cycles. Fire-resistant SiO₂ shell.

## CLAIMS

1. A TES composite of microencapsulated PCM in SiO₂ shell within expanded graphite, latent heat >200J/g, κ >5 W/mK.
2. The composite of claim 1 with PCM melting 20-35°C for building comfort.
3. The composite of claim 1 with graphite scaffold providing κ ≥25× unscaffolded PCM.
4. Manufacturing via sol-gel microencapsulation + graphite expansion + vacuum impregnation.
5. A building thermal system using panels of said composite for passive temperature regulation.
