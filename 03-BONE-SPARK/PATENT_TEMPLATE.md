## TITLE

## FIELD
The invention relates to bioactive piezoelectric scaffold, specifically bone tissue engineering utilizing KNN nanoparticles in bacterial cellulose matrix.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Stryker, Zimmer Biomet, Medtronic Infuse. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel bioactive piezoelectric scaffold comprising KNN nanoparticles in bacterial cellulose matrix achieving d₃₃ = 85-110 pC/N. Biocompatible (ISO 10993). Self-powered osteogenesis. Porosity >70%..

**Advantages:** Self-powered electrical stimulus without batteries. Biodegradable. Lead-free. Eliminates $800 external stimulators.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**KNN nanoparticles in bacterial cellulose matrix**

### Synthesis
Hydrothermal KNN (220°C, 24h) → disperse in BC culture → in situ biosynthesis (7d) → genipin crosslink → freeze-dry → pole 3kV/mm

### Properties
d₃₃ = 85-110 pC/N. Biocompatible (ISO 10993). Self-powered osteogenesis. Porosity >70%.

## CLAIMS

1. A bioactive scaffold of lead-free KNN nanoparticles in bacterial cellulose generating 0.1-5.0mV under mechanical loading to stimulate osteoblasts.
2. The scaffold of claim 1 with 5-30 wt% KNN and porosity >70%.
3. The scaffold of claim 1 with d₃₃ between 50-150 pC/N after corona poling.
4. A method via in situ bacterial cellulose biosynthesis around KNN nanoparticles.
5. A bone treatment method using said scaffold where physiological loading generates osteogenic electrical stimulation.
