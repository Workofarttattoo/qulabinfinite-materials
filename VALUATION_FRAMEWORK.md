## Executive Summary

**Portfolio Value Estimate: $1.8B - $4.2B** across 10 materials over 10-year horizon.

This framework provides three parallel valuation approaches for each material:
1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM)** — Market size opportunity
2. **Licensing Revenue Model** — Annual royalties + milestone payments
3. **Manufacturing Economics** — Cost-to-produce vs. selling price premium

---

## Valuation Methodology

### Approach 1: Total Addressable Market (TAM)

**Formula:** TAM = Total market size × Market share potentially capturable by new material

**Inputs:**
- Global market size (existing technology)
- Growth rate (CAGR over 10 years)
- Price premium achievable (new material vs. incumbent)
- Potential market share (0.5% - 5% realistic for disruptive materials)

**Output:** $ X - $ Y market opportunity

### Approach 2: Licensing Revenue Valuation

**Formula:** Annual Licensing Revenue = Sum of [Upfront Payment + (Running Royalty × Annual Licensee Sales) + Milestone Payments]

**Inputs:**
- Number of licensees per material (1 exclusive OR 3-5 non-exclusive)
- Upfront payment per license ($2M-$10M typical)
- Running royalty rate (3-5% of product sales)
- Milestone payments ($1M-$5M for major achievements)
- Time to full production (1-3 years typical)

**Output:** Annual cash flow + 10-year NPV at 20% discount rate

### Approach 3: Manufacturing Economics

**Formula:** Product Selling Price = Manufacturing Cost + (Gross Margin × Risk Multiplier)

**Inputs:**
- Raw material cost
- Synthesis complexity (affects labor + equipment amortization)
- Yield rate (%, loss in manufacturing)
- Manufacturing volume (economies of scale)
- Competitive selling price (incumbent technology)

**Output:** Unit economics + gross margin potential
