## TITLE
**Low-T Ammonia Catalyst: Ru/Ba₃SiO₄NH₂**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to low-t ammonia catalyst, specifically green ammonia (fertilizer decarbonization) utilizing Ru/Ba₃SiO₄NH₂.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Haldor Topsoe, BASF, KBR. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel low-t ammonia catalyst comprising Ru/Ba₃SiO₄NH₂ achieving NH₃ rate >5,000 μmol/g/h at 300°C/10bar (vs Haber-Bosch 400-500°C/150-300bar).

**Advantages:** 300°C vs 400-500°C. 10bar vs 150-300bar. Enables distributed green ammonia near renewables.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**Ru/Ba₃SiO₄NH₂**

### Synthesis
Sol-gel Ba₃SiO₄ → nitridation 700°C NH₃ → Ru impregnation → reduce 400°C H₂ → activate 300°C N₂/H₂

### Properties
NH₃ rate >5,000 μmol/g/h at 300°C/10bar (vs Haber-Bosch 400-500°C/150-300bar)

## CLAIMS

1. A catalyst of Ru nanoparticles on Ba₃SiO₄NH₂ for NH₃ synthesis below 350°C and 20bar.
2. The catalyst of claim 1 achieving >5,000 μmol/g/h.
3. The catalyst of claim 1 with 1-10 wt% Ru, nanoparticle size 1-5nm.
4. A preparation method via sol-gel, nitridation, wet impregnation, and reduction.
5. A distributed ammonia system using said catalyst coupled with renewable H₂.
