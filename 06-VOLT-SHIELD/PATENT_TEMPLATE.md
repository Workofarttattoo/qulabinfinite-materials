## TITLE
**Flexible Magnetic EMI Nanocomposite: Fe₃O₄/MWCNT/PDMS composite**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to flexible magnetic emi nanocomposite, specifically stretchable emi shielding utilizing Fe₃O₄/MWCNT/PDMS composite.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Laird Performance Materials, Parker Chomerics, Rogers Corporation. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel flexible magnetic emi nanocomposite comprising Fe₃O₄/MWCNT/PDMS composite achieving SE >45dB (X-band) at 1mm. Stretchable to 50%. 10K+ bend cycles. Magnetic alignment +15-20% SE..

**Advantages:** Stretchable + magnetic alignment = tunable directional shielding. PDMS biocompatible. Room-temp processing.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**Fe₃O₄/MWCNT/PDMS composite**

### Synthesis
Acid-functionalize MWCNTs → co-precipitate Fe₃O₄ onto MWCNTs → disperse in PDMS → cast → magnetic alignment during cure → 80°C cure

### Properties
SE >45dB (X-band) at 1mm. Stretchable to 50%. 10K+ bend cycles. Magnetic alignment +15-20% SE.

## CLAIMS

1. A flexible EMI composite of Fe₃O₄-decorated MWCNTs in PDMS, SE >45dB at X-band, stretchable to 50%.
2. The composite of claim 1 with magnetic field alignment enhancing SE by ≥15%.
3. Manufacturing method via co-precipitation, PDMS dispersion, and curing under applied magnetic field.
4. A wearable device incorporating said composite as conformal EMI shield.
5. The composite of claim 1 with <5% SE degradation after 10,000 bend cycles at 5mm radius.
