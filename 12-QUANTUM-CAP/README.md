## Advantages Over Incumbents
89% better than pure MXene. VO₂ adds 50% pseudocapacitance. N-Gr prevents restacking. Approaches Li-ion energy at supercap power.

## Synthesis (Summary)
HF etch MXene → CVD N-Gr on Cu, wet transfer → hydrothermal VO₂ → L-B stack → anneal 200°C Ar/H₂ → electrode fabrication → coin cell with EMIM-BF₄
