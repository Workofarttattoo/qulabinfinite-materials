## TITLE
**Woven HTS Textile: GdBa₂Cu₃O₇ REBCO tapes woven into Nomex textile**

## FIELD
The invention relates to woven hts textile, specifically flexible mri coils & fusion magnets utilizing GdBa₂Cu₃O₇ REBCO tapes woven into Nomex textile.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: SuperPower/AMSC, Bruker, AMSC. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel woven hts textile comprising GdBa₂Cu₃O₇ REBCO tapes woven into Nomex textile achieving Jc=3.2 MA/cm² at 77K. Bend radius 25mm. Weight 4.8g/m (50% lighter). Woven textile form factor..

**Advantages:** Woven = body-conforming MRI coils (prep 30min→5min). 50% weight reduction. Nomex provides mechanical robustness. Enables flexible power cables.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**GdBa₂Cu₃O₇ REBCO tapes woven into Nomex textile**

### Synthesis
Source REBCO tape → electrochemically thin substrate to 50µm → laser slit to 4mm → weave as warp with Nomex weft (3/1 twill) → Parylene-C CVD 5µm → In solder contacts

### Properties
Jc=3.2 MA/cm² at 77K. Bend radius 25mm. Weight 4.8g/m (50% lighter). Woven textile form factor.

## CLAIMS

1. A superconducting textile of woven REBCO tapes with non-superconducting weft, Jc >2 MA/cm² at 77K.
2. The textile of claim 1 with thinned REBCO substrates <60µm in twill weave with Nomex.
3. The textile of claim 1 with bend radius <30mm and <5% Ic degradation.
4. Manufacturing via substrate thinning, slitting, tension-controlled weaving, and Parylene-C coating.
5. A body-conforming MRI coil from said textile for patient-specific geometries.
