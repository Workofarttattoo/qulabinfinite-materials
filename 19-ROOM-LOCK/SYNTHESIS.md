## Protocol Summary
Arc-melt LaB₂ → load in DAC with NH₃BH₃ → compress 50GPa + laser heat 2000K → compress to 185GPa + laser heat 1500K → synchrotron XRD + resistivity in DAC

4. Reproducibility: minimum 3 independent synthesis runs
