## TITLE
**Electrospun Piezo Bioscaffold: PVDF-TrFE/Silk Fibroin/BaTiO₃ NP**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to electrospun piezo bioscaffold, specifically bone/cardiac tissue regeneration utilizing PVDF-TrFE/Silk Fibroin/BaTiO₃ NP.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Stryker, Integra LifeSciences, Medtronic Infuse. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel electrospun piezo bioscaffold comprising PVDF-TrFE/Silk Fibroin/BaTiO₃ NP achieving d₃₃=28 pC/N (2× PVDF-TrFE). Fiber 280-450nm. Degradation 2-18mo. Cell viability 96.3%. UTS 3.8MPa..

**Advantages:** Self-stimulating (eliminates $800 external stimulators). Tunable degradation. BaTiO₃ amplifies 2×. Lead-free. Silk provides biocompatibility.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**PVDF-TrFE/Silk Fibroin/BaTiO₃ NP**

### Synthesis
PVDF-TrFE in DMF:acetone → silk fibroin in HFIP → blend 70:30 + 5wt% BaTiO₃ → electrospin 18kV → pole 30MV/m at 90°C → anneal 70°C 24h

### Properties
d₃₃=28 pC/N (2× PVDF-TrFE). Fiber 280-450nm. Degradation 2-18mo. Cell viability 96.3%. UTS 3.8MPa.

## CLAIMS

1. An electrospun scaffold of PVDF-TrFE + silk fibroin + BaTiO₃ NPs, d₃₃ >20 pC/N, biocompatible per ISO 10993.
2. The scaffold of claim 1 with 20-40wt% silk fibroin for tunable 2-18 month degradation.
3. The scaffold of claim 1 with 2-10wt% BaTiO₃ amplifying piezo response while maintaining >90% cell viability.
4. Manufacturing via electrospinning of blended polymer/NP solution + electrical poling.
5. A bone treatment method using physiological loading to generate piezoelectric osteogenic signals.
