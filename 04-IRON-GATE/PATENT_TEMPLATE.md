## TITLE
**High-Entropy Alloy: CrMnFeCoNi with grain boundary carbide engineering**

## FIELD
The invention relates to high-entropy alloy, specifically cryogenic/extreme structures utilizing CrMnFeCoNi with grain boundary carbide engineering.

## BACKGROUND

## SUMMARY
A novel high-entropy alloy comprising CrMnFeCoNi with grain boundary carbide engineering achieving Yield >1000 MPa + >15% elongation at -196°C. Fracture toughness >200 MPa·m⁰·⁵..

**Advantages:** Simultaneous strength+ductility at cryo (violates conventional tradeoff). No single critical element. GB engineering adds crack resistance.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**CrMnFeCoNi with grain boundary carbide engineering**

### Synthesis
Vacuum arc melt (5× remelt) → homogenize 1200°C/24h → cold roll 80% → recrystallize 800°C/1h → optional C addition for GB carbides

### Properties
Yield >1000 MPa + >15% elongation at -196°C. Fracture toughness >200 MPa·m⁰·⁵.

## CLAIMS

1. A HEA of near-equimolar Cr,Mn,Fe,Co,Ni with special GB fraction >70%, yield >1000 MPa and elongation >15% below -150°C.
2. The alloy of claim 1 with 0.1-1.0 at% C forming GB carbide precipitates.
3. A processing method via cold rolling ≥70% + recrystallization anneal for GB engineering.
4. A cryogenic structural component for LNG, LH₂, or aerospace propulsion.
5. The alloy of claim 1 with fracture toughness >200 MPa·m⁰·⁵ at -196°C.
