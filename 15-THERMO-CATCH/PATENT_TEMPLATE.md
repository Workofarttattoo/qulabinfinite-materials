## TITLE

## FIELD
The invention relates to high-zt thermoelectric, specifically industrial waste heat recovery utilizing GeTe-AgSbTe₂ (TAGS-85) + Se doping + 2nm Ag NP inclusions.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: II-VI/Coherent, Laird Thermal, Alphabet Energy (defunct). These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel high-zt thermoelectric comprising GeTe-AgSbTe₂ (TAGS-85) + Se doping + 2nm Ag NP inclusions achieving ZT=2.1 at 300°C (60% over SoA). PF=48 µW/cm·K². κ=0.62 W/mK. S=310 µV/K..

**Advantages:** ZT 2.1 = 60% improvement. Optimal for 200-400°C industrial exhaust. All-scale scattering. SPS scalable.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**GeTe-AgSbTe₂ (TAGS-85) + Se doping + 2nm Ag NP inclusions**

### Synthesis
Melt Ge/Te/Ag/Sb/Se + 2at% excess Ag at 900°C → quench → ball mill <45µm → SPS 500°C/50MPa/5min → anneal 400°C/48h (Ag precipitate formation)

### Properties
ZT=2.1 at 300°C (60% over SoA). PF=48 µW/cm·K². κ=0.62 W/mK. S=310 µV/K.

## CLAIMS

1. A thermoelectric TAGS material with Se co-substitution and in-situ Ag NP inclusions, ZT >2.0 at 250-350°C.
2. The material of claim 1 with 3-8at% Se on Te sites and 1-5nm Ag precipitates.
3. The material of claim 1 with all-scale hierarchical nanostructuring for phonon scattering.
4. Manufacturing via melt-quench, ball mill, SPS, and controlled annealing for Ag precipitation.
5. A TEG module incorporating said material for 200-400°C industrial waste heat conversion.
