## TITLE
**Ultra-HP Thermoplastic CFRP: T1000G CF / PEKK / graphene nanoplatelet interlayer**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to ultra-hp thermoplastic cfrp, specifically aerospace primary structures utilizing T1000G CF / PEKK / graphene nanoplatelet interlayer.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Hexcel, Toray, Solvay. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel ultra-hp thermoplastic cfrp comprising T1000G CF / PEKK / graphene nanoplatelet interlayer achieving 3,850 MPa tensile. Gₗc=850 J/m² (4× epoxy CFRP). 260°C service. Thermoplastic weldable..

**Advantages:** 4× fracture toughness. Weldable = 60% fewer fasteners. 260°C service. GNP bridges cracks. Field-repairable.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**T1000G CF / PEKK / graphene nanoplatelet interlayer**

### Synthesis
Spray GNP on T1000G fiber → dry 120°C → PEKK powder deposition → AFP layup [0/90/±45]₄s → autoclave 380°C/7MPa/20min → controlled cool → C-scan NDI

### Properties
3,850 MPa tensile. Gₗc=850 J/m² (4× epoxy CFRP). 260°C service. Thermoplastic weldable.

## CLAIMS

1. A composite of T1000-class CF, PEKK matrix, and GNP interlayers, Gₗc >700 J/m².
2. The composite of claim 1 with 0.1-1.0 wt% GNP bonded to fibers via π-π stacking.
3. The composite of claim 1 with continuous service >250°C and thermoplastic weldability.
4. Manufacturing via AFP of GNP-coated tows with PEKK + autoclave with controlled crystallization.
5. An aircraft structure joined by thermoplastic resistance welding without fasteners.
