## TITLE
**Cryogenic Polymer Seal: PTFE/BNNT composite with silane interphase**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to cryogenic polymer seal, specifically lng & aerospace cryogenic seals utilizing PTFE/BNNT composite with silane interphase.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Parker Hannifin, Trelleborg, Saint-Gobain Seals. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel cryogenic polymer seal comprising PTFE/BNNT composite with silane interphase achieving Seal integrity -269°C to +260°C. CoF <0.08. Zero He permeation. No cold-flow..

**Advantages:** Full cryo-to-high-temp range. BNNT prevents cold-flow (#1 failure mode). Drop-in replacement geometry.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**PTFE/BNNT composite with silane interphase**

### Synthesis
Silanize BNNTs → cryo-ball-mill with PTFE → compression mold 50MPa → sinter 370°C/4h → machine to seal geometry

### Properties
Seal integrity -269°C to +260°C. CoF <0.08. Zero He permeation. No cold-flow.

## CLAIMS

1. A cryogenic seal of PTFE reinforced with silane-functionalized BNNTs, integrity from -269°C to +260°C.
2. The seal of claim 1 with 2-10 wt% BNNT and aminosilane interphase.
3. The seal of claim 1 with creep resistance ≥3× unreinforced PTFE at -196°C.
4. Manufacturing via cryogenic ball milling, compression molding, and free sintering.
5. A rocket propellant system using said seal in LCH₄/LOX service.
