## TITLE
**Flash Joule Heating Ceramic: SiC nanowires via FJH of Si/C precursor**

## CROSS-REFERENCE
Priority from GitHub-timestamped disclosure at github.com/workofarttattoo/qulabinfinite-materials.

## FIELD
The invention relates to flash joule heating ceramic, specifically high-temp composites utilizing SiC nanowires via FJH of Si/C precursor.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Saint-Gobain, Kyocera, CoorsTek. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel flash joule heating ceramic comprising SiC nanowires via FJH of Si/C precursor achieving β-SiC nanowires, aspect ratio >100, stable to 1600°C. Energy <$0.01/g.

**Advantages:** Seconds vs hours for CVD. No furnace. <$0.01/g energy. Nanowire morphology unique.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**SiC nanowires via FJH of Si/C precursor**

### Synthesis
Mix Si+C 1:1 molar + 5% carbon black → pelletize 300MPa → FJH 150V/500ms (~2500°C) → quench → collect

### Properties
β-SiC nanowires, aspect ratio >100, stable to 1600°C. Energy <$0.01/g

## CLAIMS

1. A method producing SiC nanowires via FJH of compressed Si/C at 100-200V for 100-1000ms.
2. The method of claim 1 producing nanowires with aspect ratio >100:1 and β-SiC structure.
3. The method of claim 1 with energy consumption <0.1 kWh per gram product.
4. A composite material incorporating said SiC nanowires as reinforcement.
5. A cutting tool coated with said SiC nanowires.
