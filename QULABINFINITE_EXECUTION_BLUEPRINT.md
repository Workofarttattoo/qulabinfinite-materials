## 🛡️ RISK MITIGATION

### Patent Risk
**Risk:** USPTO rejects claims in utility patent  
**Mitigation:** File with broad claims initially, use continuation patents for narrower claims if needed

### Market Risk
**Risk:** Licensee abandons technology before commercialization  
**Mitigation:** Structure milestone-based payments (pay as they de-risk), include buyback clauses

### Technical Risk
**Risk:** Manufacturing at scale reveals quality control issues  
**Mitigation:** Digital Twin calibration identifies issues early; include tech support in licensing

### IP Risk
**Risk:** Competitors claim independent discovery  
**Mitigation:** Provisional patent filed now establishes priority date; published work on GitHub shows prior public disclosure
