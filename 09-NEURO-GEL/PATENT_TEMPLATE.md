## TITLE

## FIELD
The invention relates to conducting neural interface hydrogel, specifically brain-computer interfaces utilizing PEDOT:PSS / alginate IPN with RGD peptide surface.

## BACKGROUND
Current solutions rely on: Blackrock Neurotech, Neuralink, Medtronic. These suffer from limitations in cost, performance, sustainability, and/or scalability.

## SUMMARY
A novel conducting neural interface hydrogel comprising PEDOT:PSS / alginate IPN with RGD peptide surface achieving Impedance <10kΩ at 1kHz. Modulus 1-10kPa (matches brain). CIC >50mC/cm². 6+ month stability..

**Advantages:** Tissue-matched stiffness eliminates chronic inflammation (#1 electrode failure cause). Bidirectional. Injectable possible. RGD integration.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Composition
**PEDOT:PSS / alginate IPN with RGD peptide surface**

### Synthesis
Mix PEDOT:PSS + alginate (60:40) → cast → CaCl₂ crosslink → 3× freeze-thaw → RGD conjugation (EDC/NHS) → UV sterilize

### Properties
Impedance <10kΩ at 1kHz. Modulus 1-10kPa (matches brain). CIC >50mC/cm². 6+ month stability.

## CLAIMS

1. A neural interface IPN of PEDOT:PSS and alginate, impedance <10kΩ at 1kHz, modulus 1-10kPa.
2. The material of claim 1 with RGD peptide covalently bonded via EDC/NHS for neural adhesion.
3. The material of claim 1 with charge injection capacity >50 mC/cm².
4. Manufacturing via ionic crosslinking + freeze-thaw IPN formation.
5. An implantable neural electrode for BCI using said material.
